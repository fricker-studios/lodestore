# Code of conduct

This project is a hobby mod with a small community around it. Keeping that community
pleasant is worth more than any single contribution.

## Expectations

- Be kind. Assume the other person is acting in good faith until it is clear they are not.
- Criticise code, designs, and ideas, not people. "This will dupe items when the hopper
  unloads" is useful; "this is garbage" is not.
- Respect that the maintainer's time is limited and mostly volunteered. A declined feature
  is not an insult, and neither is a slow reply.
- Keep it on topic. Issues are for the mod; broader arguments can go elsewhere.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, slurs, sexualised language or imagery, publishing someone's
private information, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption after being asked to
stop. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you are
representing the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete contributions that break these rules and may block
repeat offenders from the repository. Serious or repeated behaviour is reported to GitHub.

If something happens that you do not want to raise publicly, use
[GitHub's private report form](https://github.com/fricker-studios/lodestore/security/advisories/new)
with "conduct" in the title, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
