## Storage Silos
- Build with components, expands with more blocks
- Visual indicator of fullness (like redstone lamp towers, but simpler to install)
    - variable size
- Shulker unloader & loader
- Backpack unloader & loader

Initial thought was - it would be cool to have an old school storage silo (chest tower with redstone lamps). It would also be fun to correctly average the comparator signal from each chest in the array for an accurate lighting situation. I should maybe just add it to the storage mod instead of building a redstone computer to do it.

Could be a fun feature. It would require limits on the crates/backpacks which is something I kind of like.
